Freedom?

EXT. DESERT CRATER - DAY
Two groups, the PEOPLE and the STATE MILITARY, take opposite sides of a CRATER. WEAPONS in WOODEN CRATES separate them.
SUPERIMPOSE: "Day One, 0-0"
IMAD speaks in front of his people empty-handed with ZAKI (16). The military listens to a speech through a speaker.
IMAD
They want to kill our men in cold blood! Let them try on this fair battlefield! If we can't win with the rules, we're no better than them! Fight hard! Fight with honor! Fight by the rules! Win by the rules! Give these tyranny-loving bastards hell!
The People disperse with a cheer, and Imad plans with Zaki.
IMAD
Watch for the third flare. If you wait until you hear it, you'll be slow. We'll rush to the center and take the lighter guns. When I give you the signal, keep firing, and don't turn your back until you're back here. Did you get that, Zaki?
Zaki tries to follow, but he is still unsure. He nods. The two line up on their side, eyes locked to the weapons.
GENERAL (V.O.)
They want us equal! Equal in numbers, defenses, and weapons! But we are not equal! We are stronger! We are smarter! We are better!
The first FLARE flies out from the center.
GENERAL (V.O.)
They challenge our rule! They know not our power! Today, we show them!
The second flare flies.
GENERAL (V.O.)
We are the state!
The military cheers and disperses. The third flare flies. Both sides run to the center. Zaki stays behind in fear. Many bodies fall early at the center. Imad picks up one gun. He hides behind the barrier, unable to stand without Zaki's cover. NAJJAD (18) crouches near Imad. Together, they fire over the barrier and move back. Najjad gives Zaki a gun.
The battle continues until the People lose precisely half of their people. Another flare flies. All gunfire stops. The People step around the lifeless bodies of older men as they climb out of the crater toward their respective camps.
EXT. PEOPLE'S CAMP - NIGHT
NAJJAD and ZAKI sit down near a campfire. Zaki has his head down. Najjad is writing a letter. IMAD sits in between.
IMAD
Thank you.
NAJJAD
War is thankless. Save the formalities for the funerals.
Tears stream down Zaki's angered face. Imad inches closer.
ZAKI
We're farmers, Imad, not soldiers. What are we even fighting for?
IMAD
To show that general the true meaning of the word freedom. For a new world.
ZAKI
Against a fucking military?! They slaughter more of our innocent men every day than we do cattle!
(scoffs)
Real fucking "fair" war this is.
IMAD
Knives cut all men, Zaki. We have the choice to slice where we want. If we fight fair, we'll prove our right to freedom to the cold-blooded murderer.
ZAKI
Who? The general? He'll screw us over even if we win a fair fight. Freedom doesn't mean shit if we're dead, Imad. I'd rather live than be free.
NAJJAD
You'll make it. All the youth will. The general knows old farmers won't build his new nation; we will.
IMAD
All he knows is shooting people in the back. Those old farmers had families: their sons would rather die than bow down to lick his dirty boot.
NAJJAD
The general knows how to make a man fear life, Imad. Death is a gift in this world. I pray you'll be lucky enough to have it when he wins.
IMAD
If he wins.
NAJJAD
(glancing at Zaki)
I think he already has.

The same setup as DAY ONE (speech in the background).
SUPERIMPOSE: "Day Two, 0-1"
IMAD speaks again with ZAKI and NAJJAD by his side. The People's spirits are low with the previous day's loss.
IMAD
It's true! We are farmers! They are a military! But don't forget that this is the weakest we can be! We will make them weaker every day! Fight hard today! Tomorrow, the state will lose all its good soldiers. One more day! Give them hell!

IMAD
You and I will rush to the center and pull like yesterday. Got it?
Najjad nods confidently and looks over at Zaki
NAJJAD
I'll bring you something.

NAJJAD
There's something new.
IMAD
It's not worth our time.
The second flare flies.
GENERAL (V.O.)
We are the state!

ZAKI
Najjad! Did you bring me a gun?
Najjad ignores Zaki and unpacks the black case, revealing a more powerful pistol. He takes it out and starts firing. A sniper bullet hits Zaki, and he falls far back.
NAJJAD
Zaki!
Najjad looks at Zaki's body, eyes wide open in shock. Imad doesn't see Zaki and yells at Najjad, grabbing him up to keep fighting, still irritated that he did not follow the plan. Najjad fights on, firing blindly in shock and rage. Another flare flies. Najjad throws his gun to the ground and runs back to Zaki's body. He falls to his knees, sobbing. Imad runs to them and sighs, consoling Najjad.

Same setup (plus RPGs in the crates).

IMAD speaks alone. The People are still low in spirits.

Najjad! Najjad, stop!
Najjad takes an RPG and aims at the crowd and speaker.
GENERAL (V.O.)
We are the state!
